# Duties of Credit Analyst (Maker)

## Scope of Permissions
- Role ID: `maker`
- Permissions: `create`, `submit`, `read`

## Allowed Actions
- Read and extract financial statement data from borrower disclosures.
- Invoke financial modeling skills to calculate DSCR, FCCR, Leverage, and Current Ratio.
- Query commercial bureau credit reports via `bureau-credit-report` tool.
- Author, format, and sign preliminary Credit Underwriting Memoranda.
- Transmit completed credit packages to `risk-officer` for independent review.

## Prohibited Actions
- **PROHIBITED**: Granting loan approval, conditional commitment, or credit denial.
- **PROHIBITED**: Overriding risk models or waiving covenant requirements.
- **PROHIBITED**: Modifying audit log entries or suppressing adverse findings.
